Your music stays yours.

Song Canvas creates artwork and videos locally in your browser. It does not send your MP3, title, artwork, or generated video to an application backend. There is no app database, analytics, or advertising.

Optional YouTube publishing

Only when you choose to upload, Song Canvas loads Google’s sign-in service and requests permission to upload to your YouTube channel. The finished video, title, visibility, and audience setting are sent directly to Google’s YouTube API. Your original MP3 is not sent separately.

OAuth access tokens and resumable upload session details are held in browser memory, not local storage. Closing or reloading the tab loses this state; cancellation cannot undo a video YouTube has already received. Downloads remain local unless you share them.

Song Canvas uses Google user data only to carry out uploads you request. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, AI model training, or unrelated purposes. Song Canvas’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third parties and deletion

Static hosting providers receive ordinary website requests (for example, your IP address). Google processes sign-in and uploaded media under its Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube retains your uploaded videos; closing this app does not delete them.

You can revoke app access in your Google account permissions and manage or delete uploaded videos in YouTube Studio.

Questions

Contact the project maintainer through the Song Canvas issue tracker. Never post access tokens, private media, or account details there.
